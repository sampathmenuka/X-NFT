Our story

Why we built X-NFT

A studio-meets-marketplace helping artists earn a living wage while giving collectors work with provenance.

Our Story

X-NFT started as a late-night group chat between illustrators, sound designers, and devs who wanted more from web3 than speculation. We pooled savings, launched a zine, and began hosting micro-drops for friends.

Those experiments have grown into a full-stack studio: artist services, collector onboarding, treasury-backed guarantees, and thoughtful storytelling around every release.

Today we champion global creators with transparent royalties, IRL activations, and a community that votes on upcoming collaborations.

  • Dedicated curators for art, music, gaming, and photography
  • On-chain provenance audits before every drop
  • Creator care team covering legal, tax, and production support

Our Mission

Empower creators and collectors with fair contracts, sustainable revenue, and storytelling that moves culture forward.

Our Vision

Build a global home where every digital asset carries context, community, and real-world utility.
